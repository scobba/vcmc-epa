Before you read

Three things that are easy to misread, and one that is permanent.

The scale is supervision, not level

Faculty score 0–5 entrustment — "Complete Supervision" through "Aspirational" — not the ACGME's Level 1–5. The two look alike and are not the same number. Do not read a dashboard figure as the level you report in your semi-annual review.

These codes are yours alone

The residency uses 19 milestone codes and the fellowship uses 16, and 15 of them collide. PC1 means one thing for a resident and something entirely different for a fellow. The system keeps them apart; nothing here is shared with the residency.

Revise now, not later

Each EPA has a permanent id. Once the first evaluation is filed, the wording is frozen onto that record and ids can never be renamed or reused. Rewriting, merging, renumbering and deleting are all free until then — and effectively impossible afterwards.

Milestone coverage

How many EPAs feed each of the 16 Addiction Medicine milestones. Every milestone is reached by at least one, so no row will render permanently blank. Highlighted cells are reached by exactly one EPA — cut or reword that EPA and the milestone drops to zero.
